Cursor Control v287 — SHIP BODY HITBOX VISIBILITY

- Endurance ship collision now uses one shared 18x10 body-only rectangle for every spaceship. Wings, fins, glow and sprite size are cosmetic and never affect collision.

- The glow/pulse treatment remains limited to the final four rarest Garage planes; plane 12 keeps the strongest sparkle treatment.
- Single centred thruster and enhanced Silver/Gold/Rainbow micro-particles retained from v286.
