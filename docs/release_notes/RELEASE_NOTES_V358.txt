Cursor Control v358 — Survival Boss HP Bar

- Adds a dedicated boss health/progress bar above the centre bottom HUD dock.
- The bar is rendered outside the arena clipping region so it is actually visible below the playfield.
- Sentinel: 66 required successful hits = 100% HP; every valid hit removes 1/66.
- Void Serpent: 34 required core hits = 100% HP; every valid core hit removes 1/34.
- Quantum Array: 18 encounter success units = 100% HP (12 sequence-node hits, 5 vector clears, 1 final tracking lock).
- Removes numeric boss HP from the STATION HP top card; station hearts remain visible during bosses.
- Keeps all v357 HUD/QoL/profile/announcement changes.
