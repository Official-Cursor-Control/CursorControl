Cursor Control v277 — THRUSTER FIRE SIZE GARAGE

- Added FIRE SIZE as a third Space Garage tab.
- Small is free/default.
- Medium costs 250 Space Coins.
- Large costs 1000 Space Coins.
- Purchased sizes persist locally and can be re-equipped without paying again.
- Fire size multiplies both the normal Endurance thruster and the full Warp afterburner effect.
- Small = 100%, Medium = 135%, Large = 175% of the coded thruster size.
- Existing FIRE COLOR purchases and selections are unchanged.
